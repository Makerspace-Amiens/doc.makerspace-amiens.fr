## 1. Overview

Managing speaking time is not always easy, whether you are the student presenting or the teacher keeping time. This tool displays a large, clearly visible timer to follow the time elapsed during a project defence, an oral exam or a rehearsal.

For students, it is a way to practise alone and keep to a set time before the big day. For teachers or a jury, it can be projected full screen in the room during the defence, so that everyone keeps an eye on the time without checking a watch.

Just enter the planned duration: the timer counts down to zero, then keeps counting in overtime (shown in red) if the talk runs over, with a short chime at the end of the allotted time and then at every extra minute. The chosen duration is saved automatically for next time, and an **extra time** option (one third more) is available for students who are entitled to it. The timer can be paused and restarted at any moment, including from the keyboard with the space bar.
