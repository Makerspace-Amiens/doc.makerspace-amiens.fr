## What is the "MakerSpace"?

The [Makerspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackerspace) is a co-design and prototyping space within UniLaSalle Amiens. It is freely open to everyone on campus, students and staff alike.

It is a place to meet and learn, where everyone can design, make and share their projects. It follows in the footsteps of [maker culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maker_culture) and of the [FabLab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_lab) philosophy it stems from. Knowledge is therefore shared here, and we encourage people to carry out their projects autonomously: everyone can work alone or in a group on their own topic, whether personal or related to the school's activities.

Finally, the Makerspace is a strong teaching tool, firmly rooted in the school. It is meant to foster new practices in the students' education and to make teaching projects easier to run. It also supports student life and the campus clubs and societies.

## Access and opening hours

The Makerspace is **open to all every week** on **Wednesdays** from **6 pm to 9 pm** and on **Thursdays** from **1:30 pm to 6 pm**. *(For personal projects, everyone is responsible for the equipment and supplies their project needs.)*

When a supervisor is present, it is also open **Monday to Friday from 9 am to 6 pm** (on Wednesdays, subject to a supervisor being available).

A workshop may be run each week (advanced practice, introduction, discovering a technique, and so on).

The rest of the week is set aside for lab sessions and projects linked to the Makerspace.
