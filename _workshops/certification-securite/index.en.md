## Purpose

The MakerSpace gives access to machines that carry real risks: burns, cuts, chemicals, fire, laser radiation. This section gathers, for each machine or activity, what you need to know before you can use it.

It is independent of the classes you take: whether or not the machine was covered in class, the information here stays the same and up to date, whatever changes the curriculum goes through from one year to the next.

## Levels of vigilance

Access to the MakerSpace is organised in 4 levels, according to the risk associated with each machine or area.

**Level 0: Common area.** Workbenches, non-powered hand tools, project area. Open access, no prerequisite.

**Level 1: FDM 3D printing.** Low risk, unsupervised use.

**Level 2: Resin, soldering, thermoforming.** Moderate to high risk, hands-on guidance required.

**Level 3: Laser, CNC.** High risk, in-depth training and always supervised.

Each module below is independent of the others: you can read them in whatever order concerns you.

## What is not covered

This certification is about safety, not about technical mastery of the machine (settings, design, workflow). For that, see the technical sheets and tutorials of each machine, linked from each module.

## Contact

For any question about a machine, contact the MakerSpace manager or a supervisor before use.
