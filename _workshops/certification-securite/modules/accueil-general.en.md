## Who this module is for

Anyone setting foot in the MakerSpace for the first time, even for use limited to the common area (workbenches, non-powered hand tools). It is the only truly cross-cutting module: it conditions access to the space itself, before you even touch a machine.

## Why this module exists

Before talking about any particular machine, there are things to know about the space in general: where the safety equipment is, how to react in case of fire or injury, and the basic rules that prevent most avoidable incidents (tidying up, moving around, behaviour around other people's machines).

## Conditions of access to the space

The MakerSpace can only be used when a MakerSpace manager is present, or with their prior permission.

Classes take place in the space during the week. Unsupervised use must never interfere with a class in progress: it must be approved beforehand, either by the teacher running the class or by the MakerSpace supervisor present at that time.

## PPE and equipment required

No specific PPE for the common area. Each room containing MakerSpace tools has its own first aid kit, as well as a list of the equipment present and a safety sheet per machine/product. On your very first visit, find where the fire extinguishers and the room's first aid kit are, and the nearest evacuation route.

Evacuation drills are held several times a year. In all cases, follow the recommendations and instructions you are given.

## What to do in case of an incident

{% include message.html title="The absolute rule" message="Whatever the incident, immediately tell the MakerSpace supervisor present: they take charge of the situation. Your safety comes first: if in doubt about an action, do not do it; raise the alarm and evacuate rather than intervene." status="is-danger" icon="fas fa-triangle-exclamation" %}

**Fire:** never go near a fire of electrical origin to cut the power at the source. Only cut the power if a switch or an emergency stop is accessible without approaching the fire. Otherwise, raise the alarm (with the supervisor present and, if needed, by calling 18/112) and evacuate calmly to the assembly point. A fire extinguisher is only used from a safe distance, on a small fire that can be controlled, and only if you know how to use it.

**Electrical accident:** never touch the victim until the power is off. Only cut the power if it can be done without risk to yourself; otherwise, do not approach and immediately alert the supervisor and the emergency services (15/112).

**Injury (cut, burn):** alert the supervisor present; first aid only by a trained person; call 15 (SAMU, the French ambulance service) if necessary.
