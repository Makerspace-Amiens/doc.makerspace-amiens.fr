## Who this module is for

Any campus user who wants to use the CNC milling module of the SnapMaker 2.0 A350T. This safety module only covers the CNC/milling use of this machine. Its other modules (laser, 3D printing) fall under the [Laser cutting module](/workshops/certification-securite/modules/laser/) and the [FDM 3D printing module](/workshops/certification-securite/modules/impression-3d-fdm/) respectively.

CNC milling is covered technically in the second-year "digital machines" course, but this safety training is deliberately kept general and independent of the programme you follow.

## Why this level of vigilance

The CNC module is probably the most dangerous piece of equipment in the workshop in direct use: a tool rotating at high speed, with a real risk of flying debris and entanglement. Some risks do not show up straight away but build up with repeated exposure. Noise is one of them: feeling nothing at the time does not mean there is no long-term consequence for your hearing.

## What to do in case of an incident

{% include message.html title="The absolute rule" message="Whatever the incident, immediately tell the MakerSpace supervisor present: they take charge of the situation. Your safety comes first: if in doubt about an action, do not do it; raise the alarm and evacuate rather than intervene." status="is-danger" icon="fas fa-triangle-exclamation" %}

**Mechanical injury (cut, tearing):** switch off the machine immediately, do not move the victim if the injury is serious, give first aid, call 15 (SAMU, the French ambulance service) or 112 if necessary.

**Debris in the eye:** rinse thoroughly with clean water for at least 15 minutes; always seek medical advice.
