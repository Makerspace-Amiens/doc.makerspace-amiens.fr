## Who this module is for

Any campus user who has to use the band saw with a supervisor, for a specific need identified by that supervisor.

## Why this level of vigilance

The blade keeps moving continuously, including just after the end of a cut, and the workpiece or a hand getting trapped at the end of travel is a classic accident on this type of machine. That is why its use is reserved for supervisors, on request.

## Identified risks

{% include risk-item.html level="eleve" icon="fas fa-compact-disc" title="Contact with the moving blade" content="The blade keeps running even just after a cut appears to be finished. Never bring your hands near the blade until it has come to a complete stop." %}

{% include risk-item.html level="eleve" icon="fas fa-hand-fist" title="Trapping of the workpiece or the hand" content="At the end of the cut, the workpiece or the offcut can tip over and pull the hand towards the blade. Stay alert until the cut is completely finished." %}

{% include risk-item.html level="modere" icon="fas fa-burst" title="Blade breakage" content="A blade that is badly tensioned, worn or unsuited to the material can break during the cut and whip. Check the condition and the tension of the blade before use." %}

{% include risk-item.html level="modere" icon="fas fa-wind" title="Flying debris" content="Metal chips thrown out during the cut." %}

## PPE and equipment required

<div class="epi-list">
{% include epi-item.html icon="fas fa-glasses" label="Safety glasses" %}
</div>

Safety glasses are mandatory. No gloves near the moving blade, by the same logic as for the other machines with a rotating tool: a glove that gets caught pulls free less easily than a bare hand.

## What to do in case of an incident

{% include message.html title="The absolute rule" message="Whatever the incident, immediately tell the MakerSpace supervisor present: they take charge of the situation. Your safety comes first: if in doubt about an action, do not do it; raise the alarm and evacuate rather than intervene." status="is-danger" icon="fas fa-triangle-exclamation" %}

**Mechanical injury (cut, tearing):** switch off the machine immediately, do not move the victim if the injury is serious, give first aid, call 15 (SAMU, the French ambulance service) or 112 if necessary.

**Debris in the eye:** rinse thoroughly with clean water for at least 15 minutes; always seek medical advice.
