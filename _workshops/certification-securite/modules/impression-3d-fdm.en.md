## Who this module is for

Any campus user who wants to use one of the MakerSpace FDM 3D printers unsupervised.

This module can be taken as part of the first-year class that covers 3D printing, but it applies to you just as much if you did not take it in that setting.

## Why this level of vigilance

The risk is low overall on these machines, but open, lightly supervised access calls for a minimum of rigour, particularly regarding heat and the materials used.

## Allowed materials

**Fully unsupervised:** PLA, PETG, TPU.

**Any other material (ABS and others):** not usable unsupervised. Their use is overseen by the supervisors: ask before any use, so that it is done in the best conditions (ventilation, suitable settings).

## PPE and equipment required

No specific PPE in normal use. Heat-resistant gloves are recommended for removing hot parts or cleaning the nozzle.

## What to do in case of an incident

{% include message.html title="The absolute rule" message="Whatever the incident, immediately tell the MakerSpace supervisor present: they take charge of the situation. Your safety comes first: if in doubt about an action, do not do it; raise the alarm and evacuate rather than intervene." status="is-danger" icon="fas fa-triangle-exclamation" %}

**Minor burn:** cool immediately under **lukewarm water** for several minutes; never burst a blister. Let the victim adjust the water temperature themselves to whatever relieves them: neither icy nor hot.

**Fire starting:** never go near a fire of electrical origin to cut the power at the source. Only cut the power if a switch or an emergency stop is accessible without approaching the fire. Otherwise, raise the alarm (with the supervisor present and, if needed, by calling 18/112) and evacuate calmly. A fire extinguisher is only used from a safe distance, on a small fire that can be controlled, and only if you know how to use it.
