## Who this module is for

Any campus user who wants to use one of the MakerSpace laser machines (Laserbox, xTool P3, xTool F2 Ultra), alone or not. This module is mandatory: nobody uses the laser without having gone through it first, whatever their experience elsewhere.

This module can be taken as part of the first-year class that covers laser cutting, but it applies to you just as much if you did not take it in that setting, if you changed programme, or if you simply want to refresh your memory, at any point in your studies.

## Why this level of vigilance

A laser machine combines three serious risks at once: fire (the laser can ignite the material being cut), cutting fumes, and the laser radiation itself. A cut left unattended or a poor choice of material is enough to cause a serious incident, not just an inconvenience.

## Allowed and forbidden materials

**Allowed (subject to checking):** wood (plywood), paper, cardboard, acrylic.

The MakerSpace provides materials suited to the laser machines: prefer the stock supplied.

**Strictly forbidden: PVC.** Laser cutting it releases chlorine, a toxic gas: under no circumstances, even in small quantities, even "just to test".

Other materials are prohibited on some specific machines (ABS, polycarbonate, foams, HDPE, coated carbon fibre…): refer to the technical sheet of the machine you are using for the full list.

**Unknown material or doubt:** do not cut, ask first. Any material from outside the MakerSpace stock must always be approved by a supervisor before being cut.

## PPE and equipment required

No personal protective equipment is needed in normal use, with the lid closed and the interlock working. However, before starting a cut, you must know where the nearest suitable fire extinguisher (CO2 or powder) is: this is not optional.

## What to do in case of an incident

{% include message.html title="The absolute rule" message="Whatever the incident, immediately tell the MakerSpace supervisor present: they take charge of the situation. Your safety comes first: if in doubt about an action, do not do it; raise the alarm and evacuate rather than intervene." status="is-danger" icon="fas fa-triangle-exclamation" %}

**Burn:** cool immediately under **lukewarm water** for several minutes; never burst a blister. Let the victim adjust the water temperature themselves to whatever relieves them: neither icy nor hot.

**Small fire that can be controlled:** cut the power to the machine if it can be done safely, and use the fire extinguisher located beforehand.

**Fire that is growing:** evacuate, raise the alarm, call the fire brigade (18 or 112). Never try to control a fire larger than the part being cut: better to lose a part than to get hurt.

**Heavy fumes:** switch off the machine, ventilate, evacuate the area if necessary.
