## The Ottolympics

## Presentation

On Projects Day, **4 June**, every group takes part in the **Ottolympics**, a robotics tournament held at UniLaSalle Amiens to test your robots on every kind of ground!

You will have to **adapt**, **modify** and **tinker with** your **OTTO-MKS** robot to try to win the various events!

A common set of rules applies to all events, and before it can take part, your robot must go through a homologation stand to check that it complies.
