Welcome to the **Metrology** workshop!

Designing a part at the right dimension is not enough to guarantee that it will come out of the 3D printer or the laser cutter with the same dimensions. This workshop teaches you to measure properly with the basic instruments of the MakerSpace, and to check that a manufactured part matches its design.

## What you will learn

- Read and use a caliper (vernier and digital) for outside, inside and depth measurements.
- Use a protractor and a steel rule.
- Tell precision from accuracy, and avoid the most common measurement errors.
- Understand why a manufactured part is never exactly to size (shrinkage in 3D printing, kerf in laser cutting) and know how to allow for it.
- Check a 3D-printed or laser-cut part against its design.

## Why this project?

Knowing how to measure is a cross-cutting skill: it is used to check a part made in the [Additive Manufacturing](/workshops/fab-additive/) or [Laser Cutting](/workshops/decoupe-laser/) workshop, to check an assembly before putting it together, or simply to understand why a part does not fit as expected. This workshop is designed as a short, practical foundation that you can reuse in all your projects.
