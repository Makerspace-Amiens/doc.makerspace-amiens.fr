Welcome to the **Mobile robotics** workshop!

This course brings together the knowledge needed to make a wheeled robot move: how it moves, how it perceives its surroundings and how it decides where to go. It starts from the basics (motors, sensors, control) and moves on to more advanced topics (odometry, closed-loop control, autonomous navigation).

## What you will learn

- Choose and drive the actuators of a mobile robot
- Read sensors to perceive the surroundings
- Control a robot remotely
- Estimate the robot's position and control its movements
- Make a robot move autonomously

## Why this course?

Mobile robotics draws on mechanics, electronics and programming at the same time. This course is the foundation for MakerSpace projects that include a moving robot.
