**ArUco** markers are square visual codes that are easy to generate and to detect with a camera, using a library such as **OpenCV**. They make it possible to find the position and orientation of an object in space reliably and at low computing cost.

For the **Puzzle Bot**, they can be used to identify and track the puzzle pieces, or the position of the arm, if you decide to tackle the **vision** side of the project.
