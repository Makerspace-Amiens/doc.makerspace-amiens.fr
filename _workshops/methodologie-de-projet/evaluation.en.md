## What is assessed

The course assesses the **management of your project**, not its technical side: the quality of the machine, the code or the parts is assessed as part of the project itself.

The assessment is based on **the state of your repo at the end of the course**, on a date given in class (with a few days' margin after the last session). Everything in the repo on that date counts, including the history: the commit dates show whether the work was done regularly or the day before.

| Share | Weight | What we look at |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | **60%** | Your logbook and your contributions to the repo |
| **Group** | **40%** | The scoping, the organisation and the readability of the team's documentation |

{% include message.html title="Why so much individual weight?" message="The logbook is the most direct trace of what **you** did, understood and decided. It is built session after session: that is what makes documenting as you go pay off." status="is-info" icon="fas fa-info-circle" %}

## Individual share (60%)

| Item | What must appear |
|---|---|
| **Logbook: regularity** | One dated entry per session, **project and methodology**, in your `docs/journal/` folder |
| **Logbook: content** | Each entry says what was done, **why** (decisions, problems encountered) and what remains to be done. An outsider understands it without explanation |
| **Contributions to the repo** | Commits made from **your** GitHub account, regular, with messages that say what changes |
| **Taking part in the management** | Issues created, assigned and closed; reviews done for others |

See [Keeping a logbook](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/tutorials/journal-de-bord/) and [Git, GitHub Desktop and VSCode](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/tutorials/git-github-desktop-vscode/).

## Group share (40%)

| Item | What must appear |
|---|---|
| **Scoping** | `objectifs.md`: context, problem and target audience, specification with **measurable criteria** |
| **Organisation** | `equipe.md`: one lead and one backup per role. Issues and milestones used and kept up to date |
| **Traceability of choices** | The important choices are written down with the alternatives considered and the reason for the decision |
| **Readability and transferability** | Up-to-date README, no `a_modifier` / `a_supprimer` markers left, structured pages (headings, captioned figures, sources) |

See [Defining your need and your specification](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/concepts/definir-son-besoin/), [Building and organising your team](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/concepts/constituer-organiser-equipe/), [Managing your project with GitHub](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/tutorials/gerer-projet-github/) and [Recording your technical choices](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/concepts/tracer-choix-techniques/).

## Before the submission date

Go through the [project closing checklist](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/tutorials/checklist-cloture-projet/) with your team: it takes these criteria point by point.
