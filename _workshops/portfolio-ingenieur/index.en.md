Welcome to the **Engineer's Portfolio** workshop!

You are going to create your personal website: a showcase of what you have done (projects, internships, commitments) and, above all, the proof of what you can do, through your project pages, your logbook and your documentation pages. The site is built from a shared template: you only write Markdown and YAML, with no need for HTML or CSS. You can still give it your own colour. The [demo site](https://makerspace-amiens.fr/portfolio-theme/) shows what it will look like.

## What you will learn

- Create and publish a website with GitHub Pages, then develop it with Git and VSCode
- Present your background: "About me", CV, professional links
- Document a project, highlighting **your** role
- Keep a logbook and documentation pages that serve as evidence
- Keep your site alive from one year to the next, up to your internship and job applications

## Why this project?

A CV says what you have done; a portfolio shows it. For a recruiter as for the teaching team, a photo of the prototype, a diagram, a chart of measurements and a few lines about your role are worth more than a line reading "skills: CAD, electronics". It is also where you follow your progress throughout your studies.

The workshop builds on [Project Methodology](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/): what you document in the repo of your project becomes, summarised and commented, a page of your portfolio.
