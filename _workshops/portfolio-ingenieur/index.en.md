Welcome to the **Engineer's Portfolio** workshop!

You are going to create your personal website: a showcase of what you have done (projects, internships, commitments) and, above all, the proof of what you can do. Each skill of your programme is positioned on a radar chart and backed up by your projects, your posts and your documentation pages. The site is built from a shared template: you only write Markdown and YAML, with no HTML or CSS.

## What you will learn

- Create and publish a website with GitHub Pages, then develop it with Git and VSCode
- Present your background: "About me", CV, professional links
- Document a project, highlighting **your** role
- Position yourself on a skills framework and justify each level with facts
- Keep a logbook and documentation pages that serve as evidence
- Keep your site alive from one year to the next, up to your internship and job applications

## Why this project?

A CV says what you have done; a portfolio shows it. For a recruiter as for the teaching team, a photo of the prototype, a diagram, a chart of measurements and a few lines about your role are worth more than a line reading "skills: CAD, electronics". The portfolio is also the basis of the programme's skills-based approach: you follow your progress in it throughout the year, and your reviews rely on it.

The workshop builds on [Project Methodology](/workshops/methodologie-de-projet/): what you document in the repo of your project becomes, summarised and commented, a page of your portfolio.
