## Introduction

Kumiko is a traditional Japanese fine woodworking technique that consists of assembling small pieces of wood without nails or screws to create decorative geometric patterns. These compositions, often found in screens or partitions, rely on precision and the harmony of shapes.

In this exercise, we revisit this tradition in a 3D-printed, collaborative version.

## Goal

Contribute to a collective artwork installed in the PrinterLab, using 3D-printed Kumiko inserts.
