**Computer-Aided Design (CAD)** is the first step of any digital fabrication project: this is where your ideas take shape, become **precise drawings**, then **real parts**. This thematic workshop guides you step by step, from the **sketch** to the **manufactured part**, with no prerequisites.

You will discover the two main families of tools at the MakerSpace: **3D design** with **OnShape**, to model volumes and assemblies intended for **3D printing**, and **2D vector drawing** with **Inkscape**, to prepare paths intended for **laser cutting**.

## What you will learn

- **Read and interpret a technical drawing**: understand dimensions, views and conventions before modelling.
- **Model in 3D with OnShape**: from your first part to a complete assembly of several components.
- **Draw in 2D with Inkscape**: create clean vector paths, ready for laser cutting.
- **Go from the model to the material**: prepare and start a **3D print**, then a **laser cut**, on the MakerSpace machines.
- **Follow the design rules**: anticipate manufacturing constraints (tolerances, inserts, fixings) for parts that work first time.

## Who this workshop is for

This workshop is an **introduction**: it requires **no prior knowledge** of design. It is an ideal foundation to complete **before starting a more ambitious project**, such as designing a machine or a robot. Take the time to experiment, fail and start again: you learn to design by designing.
