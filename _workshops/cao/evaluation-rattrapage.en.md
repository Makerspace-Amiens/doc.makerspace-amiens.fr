## Preparing your project

For each part you make, create a new Part Studio in your OnShape project:

1. At the bottom left of your window, click the **+** button.
2. Click **Create Part Studio**.
3. Right-click the new tab, then **Rename**.
4. Replace the name of the tab with the number of the part (for example `801`).
5. Make the part in this tab.

## What is assessed

The assessment covers:

- your ability to **reproduce a part from a drawing**;
- your ability to **constrain your sketches fully and logically**;
- your **design logic and approach**: use of planes and axes of symmetry, logic of the sketches, number of features and so on.

{% include message.html title="A correct part is not enough" message="Two models can have exactly the same shape and not be worth the same mark. A fully constrained sketch (it turns black in OnShape), built on the origin planes and relying on symmetries, with a few well-chosen features, shows that you understood the part before drawing it." status="is-info" icon="fas fa-info-circle" %}

To practise before the assessment, see [Modelling practice](/workshops/cao/tutorials/drawings-training/).
