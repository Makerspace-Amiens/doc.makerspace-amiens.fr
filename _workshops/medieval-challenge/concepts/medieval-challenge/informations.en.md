## About the project

The **Medieval Challenge** is a project carried out **in pairs**, whose aim is to **design** and **make** a **miniature catapult** using the resources of the **MakerSpace**.

This project lets you try your hand at **CAD**, **laser cutting** and **3D printing**, while discovering how the place and its equipment work.

The experience ends with a **tournament** in which each catapult has to show what it can do!

## Project organisation

The project runs over 6 sessions, in three stages:

- **Project sessions (5 sessions = 15 h)**
  - Design, prototyping and making of the catapult.
  - Adjustments and validation of the prototypes.

- **Final session (1 session = 3 h)**
  - Tournament and assessment of the catapults during the event.

## Objectives

- **Use CAD** to design simple parts.
- **Export and prepare files** for laser cutting and 3D printing.
- **Design and assemble** a mechanical device that meets a specification.
- **Discover** how the **MakerSpace** works and its rules.
- **Test and compare** the designs in a tournament.

![Base holder](Base%20Support.png)

{% include step-tuto.html
greyBackground = false
title = "Energy source"
content="A **rubber band or a spring will be provided** and can be used as the energy source.
Only mechanical energy from the **elastic deformation of materials** and from **gravity** is allowed.
Any other energy source (electricity, compressed air, combustion and so on) is forbidden.
The shot must be triggered by a **50 cm cord** operated by hand."
image="energy.png" %}

{% include step-tuto.html
greyBackground = false
title = "Manufacturing"
content="Only the **digital fabrication processes available at the MakerSpace** are allowed.
**Screws and fasteners** are allowed.
Any other manufacturing technique is forbidden."
image="make.png" %}

{% include step-tuto.html
greyBackground = false
title = "Assembly requirements"
content="All parts must be fixed so as to avoid excessive play or unstable parts.
All parts must be **designed in CAD** (no improvised assembly with tape or hot glue, for example).
Mechanisms must pivot about **defined, rigid axes**.
**No part or projectile must be able to be ejected unexpectedly.**"
image="documentation.png" %}

{% include step-tuto.html
greyBackground = false
title = "Safety and projectiles"
content="Shots must be **approved beforehand by a supervisor** and fired only towards the **designated launch area**.
The materials used must have no **sharp edges** that could **injure the user**.
Only the **Nerf Rival foam balls provided** are allowed as projectiles."

{% include message.html title="Warning" message="Any **breach of these safety rules** may result in **exclusion from the class** and a **loss of points**." status="is-warning" icon="fas fa-exclamation-triangle" %}

These rules are designed so that everyone can fully enjoy this project. Each rule exists because, one day, someone behaved inappropriately and made it necessary. **Do not be that person.**

Finally, be kind to one another: **this project must remain a game!**
