## Rules of the game

The tournament takes place in **two phases.** Each **phase** consists of **4 shots** to score points. The aim is to hit a specific zone, which **changes position between the two phases**.

Teams must therefore find a way to be **accurate** with their catapult and to **choose which zone to hit**!

![playing table]({{'/workshops/medieval-challenge/concepts/medieval-challenge/tournament/table.png'}} "Playing table")

### The zones

Each **ball in the nets of a zone** adds **points to the total**:

- <span style="color:green; font-weight:bold;">Green zone</span> → **+ 10 pts per ball**
- <span style="color:gold; font-weight:bold;">Yellow zones</span> → **+ 5 pts**
- <span style="color:orange; font-weight:bold;">Orange zones</span> → **+ 3 pts**
- <span style="color:red; font-weight:bold;">Red zones</span> → **+ 1 pt**
- <span style="color:black; font-weight:bold;">Off the table</span> → **0 pts**

### The phases

The playing table changes shape slightly between the two phases, and you will have to adapt! You will have 15 minutes of preparation at the changeover to adjust your catapult.

**Phase 1:** The <span style="color:green; font-weight:bold;">green zone</span> to hit first is **1.50 m** from the launch area. The yellow, orange and red zones are spread evenly before and after this zone.

**Phase 2:** The <span style="color:green; font-weight:bold;">green zone</span> to hit first is **2.50 m** from the launch area. The zones of the other colours change slightly.

### Accuracy bonus

To reward the reliability of your catapult, **bonus** points are added if:

- **+ 5 pts** → *All the balls fired during a phase are in the **same zone***
- **+ 5 pts** → *All the balls fired during a phase are in the <span style="color:green; font-weight:bold;">green zone</span>*

Both types of bonus are **cumulative**.

### Calculating the score

The score is calculated at the **end of the attempts of the phase**, by counting the balls in the nets of each zone.

**Total score = phase 1 score + phase 2 score + bonus points**
