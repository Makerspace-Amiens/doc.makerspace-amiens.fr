## Understanding additive manufacturing

3D printing, also called additive manufacturing, consists of creating a real object from a digital model by adding material layer by layer. Far from being a futuristic technology, it has existed since the 1980s and is now widely accessible. From cutting-edge industries to makers' workshops, it offers a fast and affordable way to design, test and produce one-off parts or small series.

## A technology that serves creativity

Thanks to the variety of materials, processes (FFF, SLA, SLS, etc.) and design tools available, 3D printing has become a real driver of innovation. It makes it possible to build prototypes, repair everyday objects, or bring artistic, mechanical or architectural projects to life. It is also a great learning tool, which develops both technical rigour and creativity.

## Courses, tutorials and resources to go further

This module gathers all the teaching resources about 3D printing: theory courses, hands-on tutorials, design guides, feedback from experience and technical sheets. Whether you are a student, a teacher or a curious maker, you will find here the basics you need to understand, master and experiment with additive manufacturing in your projects.
