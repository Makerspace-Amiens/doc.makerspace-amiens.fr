## 1. Overview

SolidWorks is a commercial software application for computer-aided design (CAD), computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) and computer-aided engineering (CAE), developed by Dassault Systèmes.

SolidWorks is widely used by companies of all sizes to help them innovate and grow their business. SolidWorks products are easy to learn and use, and work together to help you design products more efficiently, faster and more cost-effectively.

SolidWorks develops and sells 3D CAD design software, analysis software and product data management software. It is part of the 3DEXPERIENCE Works portfolio, which connects your whole ecosystem, linking people, applications and data in real time across every aspect of your business.

![](Interface_SolidWorks.jpg)

Besides 3D design, SolidWorks also offers simulation, product data management (PDM) and free CAD tools (eDrawings, Composer Player, SolidWorks Explorer). These additional tools are available for download and can be added to SolidWorks to extend its capabilities.

SolidWorks is available for Windows and is licensed as a paid subscription. There is also an education version of SolidWorks, intended for students and teachers in an academic setting.

SolidWorks was introduced by Dassault Systèmes in 1995. Since then, it has been constantly improved and updated to meet the changing needs of the design and manufacturing industry.
