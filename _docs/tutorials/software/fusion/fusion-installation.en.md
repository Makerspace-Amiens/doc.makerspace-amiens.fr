## 1. Overview

Fusion 360 is a commercial software application for computer-aided design (CAD), computer-aided manufacturing (CAM), computer-aided engineering (CAE) and printed circuit board (PCB) design, developed by Autodesk.

It is available for Windows and macOS, with simplified apps available for Android and iOS. Fusion 360 is licensed as a paid subscription, with a limited free personal edition.

Fusion 360 was introduced by Autodesk on 24 September 2013. It incorporated many features of Inventor Fusion, which it replaced.

After its release, other Autodesk products were integrated into Fusion 360:

- In 2017, the Slicer feature of Autodesk 123D was integrated.
- In 2021, Autodesk Meshmixer was discontinued, after its functionality was integrated into Fusion 360
- In 2021, Netfabb was merged into Fusion 360

![](2022-09-15-10-41-54.png)

Fusion 360 has built-in capabilities for 3D modelling, simulation and documentation. It can handle manufacturing processes such as machining, milling, turning and additive manufacturing. It also has electronic design automation (EDA) features, such as schematic design, PCB design and component management.

Autodesk offers a number of paid extensions that add extra features to Fusion 360:

- Simulation
- Generative Design
- Machining
- Nesting & Fabrication
- Product Design
- Manage
- Additive Build
