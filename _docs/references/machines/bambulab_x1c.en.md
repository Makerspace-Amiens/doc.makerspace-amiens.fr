The Bambulab X1C is an advanced 3D printer aimed at high-precision projects. Its stability and reliability make it a popular choice among professional users.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 256 x 256 x 256 mm.
- **Advanced sensor system**: Optimises precision and prevents errors.
- **Compatible with a range of materials**: PLA, ABS, TPU, PETG.
- **Network connectivity**: Remote monitoring and control through a user-friendly interface.
- **Cooling and stability**: A sophisticated cooling system for flawless prints.

The X1C is perfect for users looking for a versatile, reliable printer for detailed prints.
