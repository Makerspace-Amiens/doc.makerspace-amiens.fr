## Overview

The [Cameo 4](http://silhouettefr.fr/silhouette_cameo4.html)[^1] is a cutting machine designed primarily for crafts, but it can also cut more technical materials such as copper, which makes it possible to prototype flexible PCBs. Among other things, it lets you cut vinyl, cardstock, fabric and more with precision.

Materials can be up to 30 centimetres wide and 3 metres long, and the machine can cut out printed images thanks to a registration mark detection system.
