## Overview

A fume hood (here a ductless, filtering one) draws air in through its front working opening, passes it through a prefilter and then an activated carbon filter that traps chemical vapours and aerosols, before recirculating it into the room. It protects the operator and the environment from the fumes, vapours and odours released when handling chemicals.

In the resin room, it provides additional protection when using liquid resin and isopropanol (cleaning, post-processing).

It is not equipment for large quantities of evaporated solvents, acid digestions, or chemicals of unknown composition.

## Operation

**Start-up:** switch on the lighting, check that the front sash is lowered, start the unit. A stabilisation phase of about 40 seconds follows (intermittent audible alarm): do not handle anything under the hood during that time. Once the alarm stops, the fume hood is ready for use.

**During use:** always work with the front sash fully lowered. That is what guarantees the maximum face velocity. A wide-open sash greatly reduces the effectiveness of the protection.

**Shutdown:** remove the equipment from the work area, clean the work surface, switch off the lighting, then switch off the unit.

## Limits of use

- Never use it with an unsuitable, clogged or saturated activated carbon filter.
- Never use it with the front sash wide open.
- Do not use it for large quantities of evaporated solvents, acid digestions, or products of unknown composition.
