The Bambulab A1 Mini is a compact FDM 3D printer designed for small projects while offering advanced features. It is ideal for users who want a fast, efficient solution with a small footprint.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 180 x 180 x 180 mm.
- **Compact design**: Perfect for small spaces while keeping high performance.
- **Print speed**: Optimised for quick projects.
- **Material compatibility**: Works with PLA, ABS and PETG.
- **Easy connection**: Compatible with standard connectivity options for convenient control.

The A1 Mini is ideal for makers who need a compact machine without compromising on quality.
