## Overview

A drill press holds a rotating drill bit, guided vertically by a column, to drill into a part clamped on the table. Unlike a hand drill, the part must be held still (vice, clamp) and the tool comes down onto it, which gives better precision and reliably perpendicular holes.

The MakerSpace has at least one Bosch PBD 40; other models may be available.

## Technical specifications (Bosch PBD 40)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Power | 710 W |
| Speed (1st gear) | 200 to 850 rpm |
| Speed (2nd gear) | 600 to 2500 rpm |
| Drilling capacity in wood | 40 mm |
| Drilling capacity in steel | 13 mm |
| Chuck clamping range | 1.5 to 13 mm |
| Column travel | 90 mm |
| Table dimensions | 330 x 350 mm |
| Weight | 11.2 kg |
