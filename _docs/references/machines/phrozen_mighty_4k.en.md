The Phrozen Sonic Mighty 4K is an LCD resin 3D printer, ideal for high-precision, high-resolution prints. It is suited to complex, detailed prints.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 200 x 125 x 220 mm, for medium-sized objects.
- **4K screen**: Delivers high-resolution print quality for fine details.
- **High resolution**: Able to produce layers 0.01 mm thick.
- **Stable build platform**: Gives good adhesion to avoid warping during printing.
- **UV resin compatibility**: Works with a wide range of resins for varied applications.

The Sonic Mighty 4K is particularly suited to users who want to produce detailed models with precision.
