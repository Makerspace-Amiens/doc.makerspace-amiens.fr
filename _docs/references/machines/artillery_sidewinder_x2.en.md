The Artillery Sidewinder X2 is an FDM 3D printer with a large build volume, suited to large-scale projects.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 300 x 300 x 400 mm, ideal for bulky parts.
- **32-bit mainboard**: Ensures smooth, precise print management.
- **Automatic bed levelling**: Fitted with an automatic levelling probe for simpler calibration.
- **Heated tempered glass bed**: Quickly reaches the target temperature, ensuring good print adhesion.
- **Direct Drive extruder**: Gives precise extrusion, compatible with a range of materials such as PLA, ABS, TPU and PETG.
- **Synchronised dual Z axis**: Provides better stability and precision while printing.
- **Silent stepper motors**: Reduce noise while printing, for a more pleasant working environment.
- **Filament runout detection and print resume**: These features reduce the risk of failed prints caused by a broken filament or a power cut.

These features make the Artillery Sidewinder X2 an attractive option for users looking for a reliable, capable 3D printer suited to a variety of projects.
