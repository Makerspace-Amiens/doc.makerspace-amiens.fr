The Artillery Genius Pro is an FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling) 3D printer that succeeds the Genius, with notable improvements to the user experience.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 220 x 220 x 250 mm, suited to medium-sized projects.
- **32-bit mainboard**: The Artillery Ruby board offers faster processing and better upgradability.
- **Automatic bed levelling**: Fitted with a BLTouch-type probe, it makes levelling the bed easier and so improves print quality.
- **Heated tempered glass bed**: Reaches 120°C in about three minutes, ensuring good part adhesion and easy removal once cooled.
- **Titan Direct Drive extruder**: Gives precise extrusion, compatible with a range of materials such as PLA, ABS, TPU, wood-filled filament and HIPS.
- **Synchronised dual Z axis**: Improves print stability and precision by keeping the gantry well aligned.
- **Silent stepper motors**: Reduce noise while printing, for a more pleasant working environment.
- **Filament runout detection and print resume**: These features reduce the risk of failed prints caused by a broken filament or a power cut.

These improvements make the Artillery Genius Pro an attractive option for users looking for a reliable, capable 3D printer suited to a variety of projects.
