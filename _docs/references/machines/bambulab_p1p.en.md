The Bambulab P1P is a fast 3D printer designed for prototyping and for prints on a tight schedule. It stands out for its speed and its networking capabilities.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 256 x 256 x 256 mm.
- **High speed**: Optimised for fast prints.
- **Network connection**: Allows remote control and monitoring.
- **Compatible with a range of materials**: Prints PLA, ABS and PETG with ease.
- **Efficient cooling system**: Maintains print quality even at high speed.

The P1P is ideal for those who need a fast printer for prototypes and development projects.
