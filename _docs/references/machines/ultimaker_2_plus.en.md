The Ultimaker 2+ is an FDM 3D printer known for its reliability and precision, suited to professionals and demanding hobbyists.

## Main features

- **Build volume**: 223 x 223 x 205 mm, suitable for a wide range of projects.
- **Interchangeable nozzles**: Lets you choose between different nozzle sizes (0.25, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 mm) to match the specific needs of each print.
- **Heated bed**: Reaches up to 110°C, which helps materials adhere and reduces warping.
- **Bowden extruder**: Gives precise, reliable extrusion, compatible with materials such as PLA, ABS and CPE.
- **Intuitive user interface**: LCD screen with a navigation dial for easy control of print settings.
- **Cura software**: Provides optimised print profiles and compatibility with a wide range of materials.

These features make the Ultimaker 2+ a versatile, capable 3D printer, ideal for high-quality prints.
