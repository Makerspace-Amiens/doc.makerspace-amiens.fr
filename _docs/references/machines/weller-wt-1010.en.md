## Overview

The WT 1010 is an antistatic digital soldering station, supplied with a WTP90 iron and its insulated safety rest. The tip temperature is regulated automatically and continuously, with a backlit LCD to read and adjust the setpoint.

At the MakerSpace it is used for manual soldering and desoldering of electronic components (through-hole and SMD).

## Capabilities and limitations

### a. Capabilities

- Soldering and desoldering through-hole and SMD components.
- Fine temperature adjustment to suit the alloy and the component (up to 450 °C).
- Automatic standby to limit tip wear between two uses.

### b. Limitations

- A single channel / a single iron: two different tips cannot be used at the same time on the same station.
- No built-in fume extraction: external extraction is mandatory for every use.
- No tips supplied beyond the basic kit: a suitable tip must be chosen for the component.

## Usable materials

Standard electronics solder alloys (tin-lead or lead-free) on through-hole and SMD components. Do not use it for brazing or for non-electronics materials.

## Notes

See the [safety instructions of the Electronics soldering module](/workshops/certification-securite/modules/soudure/) before any use: always put the iron back on its insulated rest and work with fume extraction.
